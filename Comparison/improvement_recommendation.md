# 3. Main Improvement Areas


Compared with benchmark practices, several areas can be further improved.


---

# Improvement Area 1

# Strengthen Educational Outcome Assessment


## Current Situation


JLU-CP activities mainly document:

- Activity implementation;
- Participant numbers;
- Activity descriptions.


However, evidence regarding learning outcomes is relatively limited.


Missing evidence includes:

- Pre-test;
- Post-test;
- Knowledge improvement measurement;
- Attitude change analysis.



---

## Benchmark Reference


HK-United collects:

- Participant feedback;
- Survey results;
- Engagement data.


These provide evidence supporting educational effectiveness.



---

## Recommendation


Establish a standardized Education evaluation process:


Before Activity:

- Conduct baseline questionnaire;
- Understand participant knowledge level.


During Activity:

- Record participation behavior;
- Collect interaction feedback.


After Activity:

- Conduct post-test;
- Analyze learning changes.



---

## Expected Improvement


Improve:

- D4 Educational Outcome Assessment


Potential benefit:

Transform Education evaluation from:

Activity description

into:

Evidence-supported educational impact.

# Improvement Area 3

# Establish Feedback-driven Iteration


## Current Situation


JLU-CP conducts activity reflection.

However, evidence of feedback-driven modification is limited.



---

## Benchmark Reference


Benchmark practices demonstrate:


Activity Implementation

↓

Feedback Collection

↓

Analysis

↓

Improvement



---

## Recommendation


Establish an iterative Education workflow:


1. Collect participant feedback.

2. Analyze educational effectiveness.

3. Modify activity design.

4. Reimplement improved activities.

5. Evaluate again.



---

## Expected Improvement


Improve:

- D5 Feedback & Iteration
