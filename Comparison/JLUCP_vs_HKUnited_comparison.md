# GEM-EduScore Benchmark Comparison Report V0.1


# 1. Comparison Overview


## Current Practice

JLU-CP 2025 Education Portfolio


## Benchmark Case

HK-United 2024 Education Portfolio


## Purpose


This comparison aims to identify:

- Similarities between current practice and benchmark characteristics;
- Existing strengths;
- Evidence gaps;
- Improvement opportunities.


This comparison does not rank teams.

The purpose is to provide evidence-based improvement suggestions.



---

# 2. Comparison Framework


The comparison follows GEM-EduScore evaluation dimensions:


D1 Goal & Audience Alignment

D2 Education Design Quality

D3 Learning Interaction

D4 Educational Outcome Assessment

D5 Feedback & Iteration

D6 Documentation & Reusability

D7 Participant Empowerment

D8 Accessibility & Inclusivity

D9 Sustainability

D10 Ethics & Responsibility



---

# 3. Dimension-level Comparison


# D1 Goal & Audience Alignment


## Benchmark Characteristics


HK-United demonstrates strong audience adaptation.

Different groups receive different educational approaches:

- Primary students;
- Secondary students;
- Elderly populations;
- General public.


The design follows:

Audience characteristics

↓

Learning needs

↓

Educational format



## JLU-CP Situation


JLU-CP demonstrates clear educational goals and activities for different audiences.


However, evidence of systematic audience research and needs analysis is limited.



## Gap Analysis


Current strength:

- Clear educational targets.


Improvement opportunity:

- Conduct audience research before activity design;
- Record participant characteristics and learning needs.



---

# D2 Education Design Quality


## Benchmark Characteristics


HK-United uses multimodal education design:

- Games;
- Books;
- Videos;
- Posters;
- Digital resources.


Scientific concepts are transformed into engaging learning experiences.


## JLU-CP Situation


JLU-CP has conducted diverse Education activities with different formats.


The activities demonstrate creativity and educational diversity.



## Gap Analysis


Current strength:

- Rich activity formats.


Improvement opportunity:

- Increase interactive and hands-on components;
- Develop reusable educational tools.



---

# D3 Learning Interaction


## Benchmark Characteristics


HK-United emphasizes interactive learning through:

- Educational games;
- Digital platforms;
- Hands-on activities.


Learners actively participate instead of only receiving information.


## JLU-CP Situation


JLU-CP includes interactive activities and participant engagement.


However, evidence describing interaction depth is limited.



## Gap Analysis


Improvement opportunity:

- Record learner participation process;
- Collect participant feedback;
- Design more learner-centered activities.



---

# D4 Educational Outcome Assessment


## Benchmark Characteristics


HK-United collects:

- Surveys;
- Participant feedback;
- Engagement data.


These provide evidence for educational effectiveness.


## JLU-CP Situation


JLU-CP documentation mainly focuses on activity implementation.


Evidence regarding:

- Pre-test;
- Post-test;
- Quantitative learning changes;

is relatively limited.



## Gap Analysis


This is one of the main improvement areas.


Suggested improvements:

- Introduce pre/post questionnaires;
- Collect learning outcome data;
- Analyze participant changes.



---

# D5 Feedback & Iteration


## Benchmark Characteristics


HK-United demonstrates a feedback process:

Activity

↓

Feedback

↓

Analysis

↓

Improvement



## JLU-CP Situation


Reflection exists.

However, evidence showing feedback-driven modification is limited.



## Gap Analysis


Suggested improvements:

- Establish standardized feedback collection;
- Document how feedback changes future activities.
