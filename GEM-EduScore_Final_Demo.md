# GEM-EduScore Final Demo


## LLM-based Intelligent Evaluation Framework for iGEM Education Practices



# 1. Project Background


## Problem


Every year, iGEM teams conduct numerous Education activities.

These activities include:

- Science lectures;
- Workshops;
- Public engagement;
- Educational tools;
- Online education.


However, current Education practices face several challenges:


## Challenge 1: Difficult Comparison


Different teams describe Education activities in different ways.

Some emphasize:

- Participant numbers;
- Activity quantity;
- Publicity.


Others emphasize:

- Educational design;
- Learning outcomes;
- Long-term influence.


Therefore, meaningful comparison between Education practices is difficult.



---

## Challenge 2: Insufficient Evidence Evaluation


Many Education reports describe:

- What activities were conducted;
- Who participated;
- What content was delivered.


However, they lack systematic evidence about:

- Learning improvement;
- Attitude changes;
- Educational impact.



---

## Challenge 3: Valuable Experiences Are Difficult to Reuse


Excellent Education practices are distributed across different Wiki pages.

It is difficult to extract:

- Successful strategies;
- Reusable designs;
- Common improvement patterns.



---

# 2. Proposed Solution


GEM-EduScore is an LLM-based Education Practice Evaluation Framework.


The core idea:


Do not directly ask AI to judge Education activities.


Instead:


Education Material

↓

Evidence Extraction

↓

Rubric Evaluation

↓

Benchmark Comparison

↓

Improvement Recommendation



The framework combines:


- Large Language Models (LLM);
- Evidence Schema;
- Rubric Evaluation;
- Benchmark Intelligence.



---

# 3. System Architecture


The system contains five major modules.



## Module 1: Evidence Extraction


Purpose:


Transform unstructured Education materials into structured evidence.


Extract:


- Education goals;
- Target audience;
- Activity design;
- Teaching methods;
- Evaluation evidence;
- Accessibility;
- Sustainability.



Core principle:


No Evidence → No Claim



---

## Module 2: Rubric Evaluation


Purpose:


Evaluate Education practices based on structured evidence.


Evaluation dimensions:


D1 Goal & Audience Alignment

D2 Education Design Quality

D3 Learning Interaction

D4 Educational Outcome Assessment

D5 Feedback & Iteration

D6 Documentation & Reusability

D7 Participant Empowerment

D8 Accessibility & Inclusivity

D9 Sustainability

D10 Ethics & Responsibility



Output:


- Strength analysis;
- Evidence gaps;
- Dimension evaluation.



---

## Module 3: Benchmark Intelligence


Purpose:


Learn from excellent Education practices.


The system extracts:


- Successful design patterns;
- Effective teaching strategies;
- Reusable resources;
- Evaluation approaches.



---

## Module 4: Benchmark Comparison


Purpose:


Compare current Education practices with excellent examples.



Output:


- Similarities;
- Differences;
- Improvement opportunities.



---

## Module 5: Improvement Recommendation


Purpose:


Generate actionable optimization strategies.



Output:


- Short-term improvements;
- Medium-term strategies;
- Long-term development directions.
