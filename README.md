# GEM-EduScore

## An LLM-based Intelligent Evaluation Framework for iGEM Education Practices


---

# 1. Overview


GEM-EduScore is an AI-assisted Education Practice Evaluation Framework designed for iGEM Education activities.


The framework combines:


- Large Language Models (LLM);
- Evidence-based Evaluation;
- Rubric Assessment;
- Benchmark Intelligence.


Its goal is to transform unstructured Education materials into:


- Structured educational evidence;
- Quality evaluation;
- Benchmark comparison;
- Improvement recommendations.



---

# 2. Motivation


iGEM teams conduct various Education activities every year.

However, current Education documentation faces several challenges:


## Challenge 1: Difficult Comparison


Different teams describe Education activities differently.

Some focus on:

- Number of participants;
- Activity quantity;
- Public influence.


Others emphasize:

- Educational design;
- Learning outcomes;
- Long-term impact.


Therefore, meaningful comparison is difficult.



---

## Challenge 2: Lack of Evidence-driven Evaluation


Many Education reports describe activities but lack systematic evidence about:


- Learning improvement;
- Knowledge change;
- Educational impact.



---

## Challenge 3: Difficult Knowledge Reuse


Excellent Education practices are distributed across different Wiki pages.

It is difficult to extract:


- Successful strategies;
- Reusable designs;
- Improvement patterns.



---

# 3. Core Idea


GEM-EduScore follows:


Education Material

↓

Evidence Extraction

↓

Rubric Evaluation

↓

Benchmark Comparison

↓

Improvement Recommendation



Instead of directly asking AI to judge an activity:


GEM-EduScore first extracts evidence, then evaluates educational quality.



---

# 4. System Architecture


The framework contains five major modules.



## 1. Evidence Extraction


Transforms unstructured Education materials into structured evidence.


Extracts:


- Education goals;
- Target audience;
- Activity design;
- Teaching methods;
- Evaluation evidence;
- Accessibility;
- Sustainability.



---

## 2. Rubric Evaluation


Evaluates Education practices using ten dimensions:


D1 Goal & Audience Alignment

D2 Education Design Quality

D3 Learning Interaction

D4 Educational Outcome Assessment

D5 Feedback & Iteration

D6 Documentation & Reusability

D7 Participant Empowerment

D8 Accessibility & Inclusivity

D9 Sustainability

D10 Ethics & Responsibility



---

## 3. Benchmark Intelligence


Analyzes excellent Education cases.


Extracts:


- Successful design patterns;
- Reusable strategies;
- Evaluation methods;
- Educational innovations.



---

## 4. Benchmark Comparison


Compares current practices with benchmark cases.


Outputs:


- Strengths;
- Evidence gaps;
- Improvement opportunities.



---

## 5. Improvement Recommendation


Generates:


- Short-term actions;
- Medium-term strategies;
- Long-term development directions.
