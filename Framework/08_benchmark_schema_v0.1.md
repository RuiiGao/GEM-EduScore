# GEM-EduScore Benchmark Schema V0.1


# 1. Purpose


The Benchmark Schema defines how excellent iGEM Education practices are structured and stored.

The purpose of the Benchmark Database is not to rank teams.

Instead, it aims to:

- Identify common characteristics of high-quality Education practices;
- Extract reusable educational strategies;
- Provide references for future Education design;
- Support comparison and improvement analysis.


The Benchmark Schema works together with:

- Evidence Schema;
- Rubric Evaluation;
- Evaluation Report.


---

# 4. Education Strategy


This section describes the overall educational philosophy.


## Education Goal


Record:

- Main educational objectives;
- Expected learning outcomes;
- Social impact goals.


---

## Target Audience


Record:

- Age group;
- Educational background;
- Community type;
- Special target groups.


---

## Education Challenge


Record:

- Problems addressed;
- Educational gaps identified;
- Motivation for organizing the activity.

# 9. Reusable Insights


The benchmark case should summarize:


## What can be reused?


Examples:


- Activity structure;
- Teaching materials;
- Evaluation methods;
- Communication strategies.


---

## Conditions for Reuse
