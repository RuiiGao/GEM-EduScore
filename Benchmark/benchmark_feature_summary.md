# Benchmark Feature 1

# Audience-Adaptive Education Design


## Related Rubric Dimensions


- D1 Goal & Audience Alignment
- D8 Accessibility & Inclusivity


## Evidence


HK-United designed different educational approaches for different audience groups, including:

- Primary school students;
- Secondary school students;
- Elderly populations;
- General public.


Different groups received different educational formats:

For younger students:

- Simplified concepts;
- Interactive activities;
- Games and models.


For elderly audiences:

- Health-related science communication;
- Cardiovascular disease awareness.



## Benchmark Insight


High-quality Education practices should begin with audience analysis.

Education design should follow:


Audience characteristics

↓

Learning needs

↓

Appropriate educational format



## Reusable Strategy


When designing Education activities:

1. Identify target audiences;

2. Analyze knowledge level and learning needs;

3. Customize teaching methods and communication styles.

# Benchmark Feature 3

# Gamification for Scientific Communication


## Related Rubric Dimensions


- D2 Education Design Quality
- D3 Learning Interaction


## Evidence


HK-United developed educational games including:

- ATGC card game;
- BIOGENIUS card game;
- Project Superbug Annihilation;
- Bacterial Clash board game.


These games transformed biological concepts into interactive learning experiences.



## Benchmark Insight


Gamification can reduce the difficulty of complex scientific concepts.

The mechanism is:


Scientific knowledge

↓

Interactive representation

↓

Learner participation

↓

Improved understanding



## Reusable Strategy


For difficult scientific topics:

- Convert abstract concepts into interactive activities;
- Encourage active participation;
- Increase learner engagement.
