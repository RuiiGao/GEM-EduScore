# 1. Education Portfolio Overview


## Original Content


HK-United designed a comprehensive educational program focusing on synthetic biology, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), cardiovascular diseases (CVDs), biotechnology, and public science communication.

The education portfolio created and delivered a wide range of educational materials, including:

- Card game ATGC
- Card game BIOGENIUS
- Computer game Project Superbug Annihilation
- Storybook DNA Way Home
- Educational posters
- Educational board game Bacterial Clash
- Educational booklet Junior Synthetic Biology
- Educational songs
- Newsletters
- VTuber model
- Redesigned paper models
- Recipes
- Pamphlets
- Skits
- Mini-lessons


The team aimed to make synthetic biology and biotechnology knowledge accessible and engaging for people of different ages and backgrounds.

The educational strategy emphasized:

- Gamification;
- Hands-on activities;
- Interactive learning;
- Digital communication;
- Multimodal educational materials.

# 2. Education Challenge


## Original Content


HK-United identified that biotechnology and synthetic biology knowledge can be difficult for the general public to understand.

The team aimed to bridge the gap between scientific research and society by making biotechnology education more accessible.

The education program targeted diverse groups, including:

- Primary school students;
- Secondary school students;
- Elderly populations;
- General public;
- Underserved communities.


Different educational approaches were designed according to audience characteristics.

For younger students:

- Simplified concepts;
- Interactive activities;
- Games and models.


For elderly audiences:

- Cardiovascular disease awareness;
- Health-related science communication.


The team emphasized that educational content should be engaging, accessible, and suitable for different communities.
