# Education Strategy

## Education Goal

### Evidence

HK-United designed an educational program focusing on synthetic biology, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), cardiovascular diseases (CVDs), biotechnology, and public science communication.  


The stated educational goals include:

- Promote synthetic biology literacy;
- Improve understanding of biotechnology;
- Increase awareness of cardiovascular diseases;
- Introduce scientific concepts through accessible approaches;
- Encourage public engagement with biotechnology.



### Benchmark Insight

**Reusable strategy: Establish multiple educational objectives combining scientific knowledge transfer, public awareness improvement, and science communication.**

This practice shows that Education projects can define goals beyond "introducing project knowledge", extending toward:

- foundational literacy building;
- disease/health awareness;
- long-term public engagement.

---

## Target Audience

### Evidence

The program targeted multiple audience groups:

- Primary school students;
- Secondary school students;
- Elderly populations;
- General public;
- Underserved communities.



Different approaches were designed according to audience characteristics:

For younger students:

- Simplified concepts;
- Interactive activities;
- Games and models.

For elderly audiences:

- Cardiovascular disease awareness;
- Health-related science communication.



### Benchmark Insight

**Reusable strategy: Design education according to audience characteristics rather than applying one universal teaching method.**

Transferable principle:

> Audience segmentation → learning needs analysis → customized educational format.

---

## Education Challenge

### Evidence

HK-United identified that biotechnology and synthetic biology knowledge can be difficult for the general public to understand.

The team aimed to bridge the gap between scientific research and society by making biotechnology education more accessible.



### Benchmark Insight

**Reusable strategy: Start education design from the communication gap between scientific knowledge and target communities.**

The educational challenge is not only "lack of knowledge", but also:

- difficulty understanding scientific concepts;
- barriers between researchers and public audiences.
